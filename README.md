2. **About (`/about`)**: Background, core philosophy (*"Driven by curiosity, focused on innovation, and passionate about bringing imagination to life through technology"*), and engineering methodology.

5. **Research (`/research`)**: Scientific publication showcase with research domains, ongoing initiatives, preprints registry, and conference participation.
6. **Experience (`/experience`)**: Vertical timeline featuring upcoming AI internship at Internmo and previous web development internship at NVSKZEN.
7. **Education (`/education`)**: Academic credentials (B.Tech AI & ML at Vel Tech with 9.6 CGPA, Diploma with 98%, Secondary with 80%).
8. **Achievements (`/achievements`)**: Honors including Karate Black Belt, National Karate Champion, and Smart India Hackathon (SIH) 2026.
9. **Contact (`/contact`)**: Direct contact channels, copy email functionality, and working mailto dispatch fallback.

## 🛠️ Technology Stack

- **Framework:** React 18 + TypeScript
- **Styling:** Tailwind CSS (Custom Dark AI Engineering Theme)
- **Icons:** Lucide React
- **Routing:** Type-safe, zero-dependency HTML5 client router with hash fallback
- **Visuals:** Canvas-driven neural background nodes and interactive SVG workflow diagrams
